HR Hospital Management System

Comprehensive Solution for Medical Facility Operations

The HR Hospital module is a robust Odoo 19-ready application designed to streamline hospital administration. From managing doctor schedules to tracking complex patient medical histories, this module provides all the tools necessary for a modern healthcare environment.

Key Features

Medical Staff Management

  • Hierarchical Mentorship (Doctors & Interns)
  • Automated Experience Calculation
  • Professional Specialty Classification
  • License and Education Tracking

Patient Care & History

  • Electronic Medical Records (EMR)
  • Advanced Visit Scheduling & Rescheduling
  • Hierarchical Disease Classifier (ICD-10)
  • Automated Patient Doctor History

Advanced Workflows

Schedule Wizard

Bulk generate doctor shifts for weeks with parity (even/odd) support.

Data Export

Export patient cards to JSON or CSV formats for external portability.

Security & Roles

Multi-level access: Patient, Intern, Doctor, Manager, and Admin.
